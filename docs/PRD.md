# PRD: ProjectScout

## Overview

ProjectScout helps students discover realistic app and website projects based on current problems discussed online. Users describe their interests, skills, and constraints; the product researches public sources and proposes three evidence-backed project ideas.

## Target User

Students and beginner developers looking for portfolio, coursework, or hackathon projects they can realistically complete.

## Problem

Generic AI idea generators frequently produce repetitive ideas without evidence of real demand. Students also struggle to judge whether an idea matches their skills and available time.

## MVP User Flow

1. User enters a topic, skills, difficulty, team size, and available time.
2. The app searches recent public online sources.
3. It groups recurring problems and generates three project ideas.
4. The user compares the ideas and opens a detailed project brief.

## Core Features

- Guided project-research form
- Online research with visible source links and dates
- Three differentiated project recommendations
- For each idea:
  - Target user and problem
  - Supporting evidence
  - Proposed solution
  - MVP feature list
  - Difficulty and estimated scope
  - Similar products and differentiation
  - Risks and validation experiment
- Clear warning when supporting evidence is weak

## Non-Goals

- Guaranteeing that an idea is completely unique
- Predicting commercial success
- Producing full business plans
- Automatically building the suggested application
- Scraping restricted platforms without permission

## Success Metrics

- At least 80% of generated ideas contain three or more relevant sources.
- At least 60% of users select or save one suggested idea.
- Fewer than 5% of factual claims contain broken or unsupported citations.
- Most research reports complete within 90 seconds.

## Product Principles

- Evidence must be separated from AI interpretation.
- Never invent market sizes, quotes, or demand statistics.
- Prefer three well-researched ideas over many generic suggestions.
- Recommendations must respect the student’s skills, time, and experience.
